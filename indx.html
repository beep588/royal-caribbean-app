Stateroom #4030 Deck 4 • Forward/Midship

Your Home at Sea on Serenade of the Seas

Reservation #5527483 • Radiance Class Vessel

Prime Deck 4 Location Perks

Stateroom 4030 on Deck 4 places you on the same level as the lower Tropical Theatre (forward) and the Reflections Main Dining Room (aft), plus just one quick flight of stairs from the Centrum lobby and outdoor Promenade Deck (Deck 5)!

Low-Deck Motion Stability & Gangway

Being on Deck 4 closer to the waterline means noticeably less side-to-side pitch and roll during windy northbound Pacific sea days (Days 6 & 7). You are also closest to the lower gangway and Cabo tender launch platforms!

Stateroom 4030 Power & Wall Hacks

Serenade of the Seas staterooms have limited standard US 110V outlets at the vanity desk. Bring a non-surge-protected USB charging block for your DSLR & GoPro batteries, and use heavy-duty magnetic hooks on the steel cabin walls!

Serenade of the Seas Features to Pack For

Radiance-class glass walls & outdoor deck activities

  • Rock Climbing Wall (Deck 13): Requires dry athletic socks and shorts/t-shirt. We included athletic socks in your clothing checklist!
  • Adults-Only Solarium & Pool Deck (Deck 11): Glass-canopied tropical pool area—bring 2–3 swimsuits and towel clips for breezy upper decks.
  • Helipad Bow Access (Deck 5 Forward): Walk out to the very front bow of the ship during San Diego sail-away (past Point Loma & NAS North Island) and Cabo arrival!
  • Safari Club & Panoramic Glass Elevators: Amazing aft wake views for spotting whales and dolphins with your binoculars on Sea Days 3, 6 & 7.

Dietary, Port & Prohibited Items Reminders

Smooth sailing tips for Mexico & Royal Caribbean

  • Gluten-Free Dining & Port Snacks: Reflections Dining Room (Deck 4) & Windjammer Marketplace offer dedicated gluten-free options. For shore days in Ensenada, Cabo, and La Paz, only bring factory-sealed gluten-free snacks off the ship (no loose fruit or open food allowed through Mexican agricultural inspection).
  • No Surge Protectors or Clothing Irons: Royal Caribbean strictly prohibits surge-protected power strips and travel steamers/irons. Pack a non-surge USB hub and wrinkle-release spray instead.
  • Keep Watch on "Ship Time": In Cabo San Lucas and La Paz, always keep your watch or phone locked to Serenade of the Seas ship time so you never miss the last tender boat!
